⚖️ KPI Scoring Weights (Ops Head only)
Define how performance is measured. Set weights for each factor — they must total 100%.
Total 100%
How scoring works:
• Compliance — % of working days with a KPI log filed
• Consistency — current filing streak (rewards habit)
• Volume — total items calibrated (quantity)
• Complexity — weighted items based on job type you define
• Quality — jobs with no rejection or rework

Set Volume and Complexity low if your team does precision work — a 10-item complex job scores equal to 50 simple items.
